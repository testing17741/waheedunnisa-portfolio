Guidelines for referencing publications, curriculum diagrams, and research datasets.

1. Scholarly Attribution & Citation

Academic research papers listed on this portal (including the JETIR publication on marigold ensilage and pedagogical studies) may be freely cited in scholarly literature, student theses, and educational reviews under standard academic fair use with appropriate attribution to the authors: Waheedunnisa Chaudri and co-authors.

2. Published Textbooks & Manuals

The textbook “B. Sc. Semester II, Major Microbiology Course (Practical)” is protected under copyright by My Rays Publishing House (Pune) and the contributing authors. No unauthorized commercial reproduction, redistribution, or electronic mirroring is permitted without express written publisher consent.

3. Mind Maps & Pedagogical Materials

The conceptual mind-mapping diagrams and pre-lab worksheets developed for undergraduate microbiology teaching may be adapted for non-commercial classroom instruction with proper credit given to the author and SIES College of Arts, Science and Commerce.

4. Disclaimer of Academic Advice

Research content and experimental protocols are shared for scientific inquiry and academic purposes. Implementation of laboratory cultures, ensilage protocols, or dietary trials should be performed in properly equipped biosafety level (BSL) compliant facilities following institution safety standards.
