Privacy Policy & DPDP Notice

Effective Date: October 2026 • Compliant with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

1. Data Fiduciary Identity

This academic portfolio is maintained by Ms. Waheedunnisa Nizamuddin Chaudri, Assistant Professor and Ph.D. Research Scholar in the Department of Microbiology at SIES College of Arts, Science and Commerce (Autonomous), Sion West, Mumbai, India.

2. Personal Data Collected & Purpose

We collect personal information exclusively when you voluntarily submit the academic inquiry form:

  • Full Name: To address you professionally in correspondence.
  • Email Address: To reply to your academic query or invitation.
  • Institution / Affiliation: To contextualize research collaboration proposals.
  • Inquiry Subject & Message: To evaluate your academic inquiry.

We do not sell, rent, commercialize, or share your submitted personal data with third-party advertising brokers or tracking aggregators.

3. Legal Basis & Explicit Consent (DPDP Act 2023)

Processing is conducted solely on the legal basis of explicit opt-in consent provided by you when submitting the contact form, in adherence with Section 6 of the DPDP Act 2023. You may withdraw consent at any time by requesting deletion of your correspondence record.

4. Data Minimization & Retention

Contact submissions are retained strictly for the duration necessary to address your academic communication (maximum 90 days following inquiry resolution), after which records are purged from administrative logs.

5. Rights of the Data Principal

Under the DPDP Act 2023 and applicable privacy standards, you hold the right to:

  • Request a summary of the personal data held about you.
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate information.
  • Request the erasure of your submitted correspondence data.
  • Nominate an individual to exercise rights in the event of incapacity.

6. Data Grievance Contact

For any inquiries or erasure requests regarding personal data, you may reach out via the official contact channel addressed to Ms. Waheedunnisa Chaudri, Department of Microbiology, SIES College, Sion West, Mumbai - 400022.
