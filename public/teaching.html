Courses Taught

Undergraduate Microbiology Modules

Current and core courses instructed within the Department of Microbiology at SIES College.

MIC-301 B.Sc. Semester III

Microbial Physiology & Metabolic Pathways

Detailed instruction in bacterial growth kinetics, Monod equations, continuous chemostat dynamics, fermentative pathways, respiratory electron transport systems, and allosteric metabolic regulation in prokaryotes.

Pedagogy: Concept Flowchart Workshops
MIC-402 B.Sc. Semester IV

Environmental Microbiology & Waste Management

Applied coursework studying municipal solid waste degradation, temple flower organic waste valorization, anaerobic ensilage fermentation kinetics, cellulolytic microbial succession, and bio-fertilizer production.

Pedagogy: Temple Waste Silage Field Protocol
MIC-503 B.Sc. Semester V

Applied Industrial Microbiology & Fermentation

Bioreactor design, upstream media formulation from agricultural by-products, aeration/agitation controls, and downstream product recovery of bacterial enzymes, carotenoids, and organic acids.

Pedagogy: Virtual Fermentor Simulations
MIC-201P B.Sc. Semester II

Microbiology Core Laboratory Practicals

Foundational bench training: aseptic transfer, pure culture preservation, Gram and endospore differential staining, serial dilution plate counts, and spectrophotometric growth measurement.

Pedagogy: Practical Manual by My Rays Publishing
Interactive Framework

The Three-Stage Experiential Model

Explore the research framework published on ResearchGate that demonstrated a 38% decrease in student procedural bench contamination and measurable gains in long-term retention.

PHASE 01

Pre-Laboratory Mapping

Structured visual concept diagrams connect metabolic principles to bench steps before handling live bacterial cultures.

PHASE 02

Active Bench Inquiry

Troubleshooting decision trees empower undergraduate students to identify contamination causes independently.

PHASE 03

Formative Synthesis

Micro-quizzes and structured reflections achieve statistically significant gains in 60-day concept recall.
